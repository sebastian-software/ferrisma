# WP10: VP8 encoder core

- Status: every unit of the WP10 inventory is translated, except the `Init`
  functions that only assign portable pointers and the compile-time branches
  that the reference configuration does not build (not-applicable checks), and
  the SIMD variants (wave 4). Driven step by step in the order of `WebPEncode`,
  the encoder writes byte-identical files to the C oracle's `WebPEncode` on
  every compared case, with and without alpha, single-threaded and threaded.
- Sub-wave: 2c
- Model: Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`)
- Depends on: WP1, WP2, WP4, WP6, WP9, WP11 (all merged)
- Probes: `tools/oracle/probes/wp10/`; tests: `tests/wp10_*.rs`, which reuse the
  image generators of `tests/support/wp11_images.rs`

Written by the package session; the integrator aggregates it into
[PORT_STATUS.md](../PORT_STATUS.md). See the package's row in
[WORKPLAN.md](../WORKPLAN.md) for its verification inputs. The inventory
comes from `tools/units.json`, including the function- and type-level
exceptions inside a module.

## Decisions applied

The integrator settled these before the translation; deviations 1 to 7 record
how they are applied.

- **Encoder context per call**, as WP11 did for `VP8LEncoder`: `VP8Encoder`
  stores neither `config` nor `pic`, and `VP8EncIterator` does not hold `enc`.
  Every function that reads them takes them per call.
- **The alpha worker receives only owned data** (ADR 0003, "Threads", the WP6
  case): the copy of the alpha plane is made on the calling thread before
  `Launch`, and the results come back at `Sync`.
- **DSP after WP4's pattern**: plain functions over the `_C` code, static tables,
  predictors over slices with positions.
- **Token buffer** as a vector of pages with C's page size and order.
- **Floating point** in source order with C's widths and libm functions.
- **Statistics**: the fields that `StoreStats` (WP13) copies stay encoder
  fields; where C writes `pic->stats` directly, the Rust code does too.

## Not-applicable checks

1. **SIMD variants.** `enc_sse2.c`, `enc_sse41.c`, `enc_neon.c`, `cost_sse2.c`,
   `cost_neon.c` and `IsFlat_NEON` of `quant.h` are wave 4 (WP19); the
   reference configuration compiles none of them, so `IsFlat` is `IsFlat_C`.
   MIPS and MSA variants are excluded (`tools/units.json`).
2. **`Init` functions.** `VP8EncDspInit` (with its call of `VP8DspInit`),
   `VP8EncDspCostInit`, `VP8FiltersInit`, `VP8SSIMDspInit` and
   `WebPInitAlphaProcessing` only assign portable pointers and have no Rust
   counterpart (ADR 0003); their calls are comments. `InitTables` fills `clip1`
   once, which `CLIP1` does on first use.
3. **Compile-time switches off in the reference configuration.**
   `WEBP_DISABLE_STATS`, `WEBP_REDUCE_SIZE`, `DISABLE_TOKEN_BUFFER`,
   `PRINT_MEMORY_INFO`, `DEBUG_BLOCK` and `WEBP_MSAN` are undefined, and
   `SEGMENT_VISU` and `DEBUG_SEARCH` are 0, so their branches are not translated.
   `USE_TDISTO` is 1 and `USE_LEVEL_CODE_TABLE` is defined, so only those
   variants are translated. `DO_TRELLIS_UV` is 0; its branch is translated and
   never taken.
4. **`WebPEncode`, `GetPSNR`, `FinalizePSNR`, `StoreStats`** are WP13's; the
   encode test mirrors `WebPEncode`'s calls and `StoreStats`'s copy as test code.
5. **Allocation failures.** The size tests of `WebPSafeMalloc` are translated
   with the C totals, but no generated input exceeds `WEBP_MAX_ALLOCABLE_MEMORY`
   (that needs dimensions above `WEBP_MAX_DIMENSION`), and host allocation
   failures are not injected.
6. **Floating point.** `pow` in `QualityToCompression`,
   `QualityToJPEGCompression` and `VP8SetSegmentParams`, the `float` pass
   statistics of `frame_enc.c`, `log10` in this file's `GetPSNR`, and the
   `double` SSIM sums of the filter search are compared bit for bit on this host
   (ARM64, Apple clang, `-ffp-contract=off`). Rust's `f64::powf` and
   `f64::log10` call the platform libm, as C does; equality on other hosts is
   recorded, not assumed (PORTING).
7. **32-bit C size.** `VP8Encoder::C_SIZE` is 23,704 on the 64-bit hosts, which
   the constants probe confirms, and 22,680 for i386 (from the C compiler's
   i386 target); 32-bit targets are not run here.
8. **`NULL` arguments.** `DeleteVP8Encoder(NULL)` and the other `NULL` cases exist
   only at the C ABI (PORTING rule 8).
9. **Assertions** are `debug_assert!`; `assert(sizeof(header) == ALPHA_HEADER_LEN)`
   is a constant assertion.

## Recorded deviations

Deviations are changes of representation that the ADRs allow, or behavior that
the Rust side cannot reproduce exactly. Each records the reason.

1. **Encoder context per call.** The encoder functions take
   `config: &WebPConfig` and `pic: &mut WebPPicture` where C reads `enc->config`
   and `enc->pic`: `init_vp8_encoder(config, picture)`, `vp8_enc_analyze`,
   `vp8_enc_loop`, `vp8_enc_token_loop` (`enc, config, pic`), `vp8_enc_write(enc,
   pic)`, `vp8_enc_init_alpha(enc, pic)`, `vp8_enc_start_alpha(enc, config, pic)`,
   `vp8_enc_finish_alpha(enc, pic)`, `vp8_enc_delete_alpha(enc, pic)` and
   `delete_vp8_encoder(enc, pic)`, which needs the picture because the alpha
   worker's results reach it at `Sync`. The iterator functions take the encoder
   (`&VP8Encoder` or `&mut`), the picture or the configuration that they read in
   C; `VP8IteratorProgress` takes the picture and the encoder's `percent`, the two
   things C's `WebPReportProgress(enc->pic, percent, &enc->percent)` touches.
2. **Encoder memory.** C carves `mb_info`, `preds`, `nz`, `lf_stats`, `y_top`,
   `uv_top` and `top_derr` from one block behind the encoder; here they are
   vectors, allocated with a fallible reservation after the size test of
   `WebPSafeMalloc` on C's total (with `sizeof(VP8Encoder)` and the
   `WEBP_ALIGN_CST` padding). The pointers are positions: C's `enc->preds` is
   `preds_base()` in the whole `preds` plane, `enc->nz` is position 1 of `nz`
   (`nz[-1]` is position 0), and `uv_top` is a position in `y_top`. `lf_stats` and
   `top_derr` are `Option` (C's `NULL`), `proba` is boxed, and `InitVP8Encoder`
   returns `Result<Box<VP8Encoder>, WebPEncodingError>`. Rust zero-initializes the
   arrays that C leaves uninitialized; every element is written before it is
   read on the compared inputs.
3. **Iterator.** `VP8EncIterator` holds positions instead of pointers: `mb`, `bw`,
   `preds`, `nz`, `y_top`, `uv_top` into the encoder, `y_left`, `u_left`,
   `v_left` into `yuv_left_mem`, `i4_top` into `i4_boundary`. `yuv_in`, `yuv_out`,
   `yuv_out2` and `yuv_p` are four buffers instead of four parts of `yuv_mem`, so
   `SwapOut` swaps two buffers. A function that C gives a destination inside the
   iterator (`ReconstructIntra16`, `ReconstructUV`, the loop of `PickBestIntra4`)
   gets the buffer moved out of the iterator for the call. The 32-byte scratch
   that the analysis passes to `VP8IteratorImport` is the iterator's `tmp_32`, and
   `top_in_tmp_32` says whether `y_top` and `uv_top` point there (C's argument is
   a flag here). `lf_stats` and `top_derr` record whether the encoder has them.
   `i4_boundary` has 40 entries on AArch64 and 37 elsewhere, as C's
   `WEBP_AARCH64 && BPS == 32` decides. `Default` gives zeroed buffers where C
   declares an uninitialized iterator.
4. **Mode setters.** `VP8SetIntra16Mode`, `VP8SetIntra4Mode`, `VP8SetIntraUVMode`,
   `VP8SetSkip` and `VP8SetSegment` write through `ModeRows`, the macroblock infos
   and intra modes that C reaches through `it->mb` and `it->preds`: the encoder's
   whole arrays (`ModeRows::whole`), or the rows of one analysis job. C's bit-fields
   of `VP8MBInfo` are `u8` fields, and the setters keep the low bits as a bit-field
   assignment does.
5. **Threaded analysis (ADR 0003, threads: a worker inside one call).** With
   `thread_level > 0` and at least two split rows, `VP8EncAnalyze` runs the side
   `SegmentJob` on a `std::thread::scope` thread and the main job on the calling
   thread, as C's `Launch` and `Execute` do; thread creation failure is C's failed
   `Reset`, and the join is the two `Sync` calls, a panicking side job counting as
   a failed hook. The jobs share the encoder read-only; `mb_info`, `preds` and
   `percent` leave the encoder for the time of the jobs, and `mb_info` and `preds`
   are split at the side job's first row, so each job writes only its rows (with
   two macroblock rows the side job has no rows, and its first position, one past
   the plane, is clamped). The picture's callbacks are not `Send`, so the jobs
   work on pictures rebuilt from the planes (`SourcePlanes`); the main job's
   picture gets a hook that forwards to the picture's hook, which leaves the
   picture for that time, and its error code is recorded on the picture after the
   join. The single-thread path runs the job on the picture itself. Only the main
   job reports progress, in C too.
6. **Alpha worker (ADR 0003, threads: a worker that outlives one call).** The
   worker's job is `VP8EncAlphaJob`: the copy of the alpha plane without stride
   (the first step of C's `EncodeAlpha`, made on the calling thread before
   `Launch`; `None` when its allocation fails, which the job reports as C would),
   the dimensions, `method`, `alpha_quality`, `alpha_compression` and
   `alpha_filtering`, a copy of the picture's statistics, and the results: the
   alpha data and size, `sse[3]`, the job's error code and statistics. They move
   into the encoder and the picture at `Sync` in `VP8EncFinishAlpha`, or in
   `VP8EncDeleteAlpha` when the loop failed; only the ten statistics fields that
   C's job writes are copied back, and the main thread writes none of them
   meanwhile. C sets the hook in `VP8EncInitAlpha`; the job is set at
   `VP8EncStartAlpha`, after `Reset`, because it carries the plane. In place
   (`thread_level` 0) the same job runs in `VP8EncStartAlpha` and its results move
   at once, which is C's order exactly. With the thread, C's worker records its
   error on the picture whenever it fails, racing with the main thread; the Rust
   job's error is recorded at `Sync`, after any error of the main thread. The
   order only matters when both fail, which needs an allocation failure in the job.
7. **Residuals.** `VP8Residual` borrows the probabilities, statistics and costs of
   one coefficient type of the encoder's `proba` (statistics mutably) and the 16
   levels of the current block. `VP8InitResidual` takes `&mut VP8EncProba`, all C
   reads of `enc`, and returns a residual without levels; `VP8SetResidualCoeffs`
   gives it its levels, as in C.
8. **Remapped costs.** C stores in `remapped_costs[type][n][ctx]` a pointer to the
   row `level_cost[type][band][ctx]`; the Rust table stores `band`, and
   `CostArrayPtr::row(n, ctx)` returns the row (C: `costs[n][ctx]`).
9. **Trellis.** For `n == 15`, `TrellisQuantizeBlock` reads `costs[16][ctx]`, one
   entry past the remapped table, and never uses the value; the Rust code stores a
   placeholder row there. Dead and terminal nodes are otherwise as in C.
10. **Token pages.** `VP8TBuffer` keeps its pages in a vector in the order of C's
    `next` chain; `last_page` and `tokens` are the last page. `TBufferNewPage`
    applies the size test of `WebPSafeMalloc(1, sizeof(VP8Tokens) + page_size *
    sizeof(token_t))` before it reserves the page. `VP8EmitTokens` with
    `final_pass` empties the vector, where C frees the pages and sets `pages` to
    `NULL`.
11. **Pointer swaps of the mode search.** `PickBestIntra16` tracks which of its two
    scores is the best (C swaps `rd_cur` and `rd_best`); `PickBestUV` tracks which
    output buffer holds the best block (C swaps `dst` and `tmp_dst`). `PickBestIntra4`
    reconstructs its trials into a local 4-row block instead of `yuv_p + I4TMP`,
    a scratch area of the prediction buffer that nothing else reads, and tracks
    whether the best block is there or in `yuv_out2` (C swaps `tmp_dst` and
    `best_block`).
12. **Chroma filters of `DoFilter`.** The decoder's chroma filters (WP6) take the U
    and V planes as two buffers; `yuv_out2` holds U and V side by side in each row,
    which no split separates. The V samples are filtered in a copy of the buffer
    and copied back; each filter touches only its own plane's columns, so the
    samples are C's.
13. **DSP forms.** Predictors take the destination buffer, `left` as
    `Option<(&[u8], usize)>` (the buffer and the position of `left[0]`, because C
    reads `left[-1]`), and `top` as `Option<&[u8]>`; `None` is C's `NULL`.
    `VP8EncPredLuma4` takes the boundary and the position of `top[0]`, because C
    reads `top[-5]` to `top[7]`. `VP8Mean16x4` writes four entries of a slice.
    Transforms and quantizers take slices from the block's first element.
14. **Iterator import and export.** A row start before the start of its plane
    panics, as in WP9 (deviation 7 there); C reads memory before the pointer,
    which only a negative stride produces. `VP8IteratorExport` writes into the
    picture's planes and panics on a shared view.
15. **Return types (ADR 0003, errors).** Functions whose every failure records an
    error return `Result` with the recorded code: `InitVP8Encoder`,
    `VP8EncAnalyze`, `VP8EncLoop`, `VP8EncTokenLoop`, `VP8EncWrite`,
    `VP8IteratorProgress`, `PreLoopInitialize`, `PostLoopFinalize`,
    `PutWebPHeaders`, `EmitPartitionsSize`, `GeneratePartition0`. Functions with a
    failure that records no code stay `bool`: `VP8EncStartAlpha`,
    `VP8EncFinishAlpha`, `VP8EncDeleteAlpha`, `DeleteVP8Encoder`, `StatLoop`,
    `RecordTokens`, `VP8EmitTokens`, and the alpha helpers. `VP8Decimate` returns
    whether the macroblock is skipped as `bool`.
16. **Writer.** A missing writer panics where C calls `NULL`, as in WP11.
17. **Allocation helpers.** `SmoothSegmentMap`, `ApplyFiltersAndEncode` and the
    alpha copy apply `WebPSafeMalloc`'s size test and reserve fallibly; the
    buffers are zeroed where C leaves them uninitialized, and every element is
    written before it is read.
18. **Lint expectations.** Item-level `#[expect]`s name the C construct (ADR 0005):
    the truncating, wrapping and sign-changing conversions of C, C names
    (`similar_names`, `many_single_char_names`), C condition forms (`if_not_else`,
    `bool_to_int_with_if`, `collapsible_if`, `identity_op`, `manual_checked_ops`),
    C index loops (`needless_range_loop`), pointer parameters of small structs
    (`trivially_copy_pass_by_ref`), the flags of `VP8EncIterator`
    (`struct_excessive_bools`) and functions translated whole (`too_many_lines`).

## Undefined behavior excluded from the oracle

Rule 8 of PORTING: the C code has undefined behavior on these inputs, so they are
excluded from the comparison, in every variant.

1. **Uninitialized lossless statistics of uncompressed alpha.** With
   `alpha_compression` 0, `EncodeAlphaInternal` never writes the statistics of its
   `FilterTrial`, an uninitialized stack object, and `ApplyFiltersAndEncode`
   copies them into `pic->stats`. The encode tests do not request statistics for
   pictures with alpha and `alpha_compression` 0; Rust copies zeros.
2. **Uninitialized bit counts in `extra_info` type 6.** `StoreSideInfo` reads
   `it->luma_bits` and `it->uv_bits`, which only `CodeResiduals` writes: the token
   loop never does, and `VP8EncLoop` not before the first coded macroblock. Type 6
   is compared only for `VP8EncLoop` on noise pictures, whose first macroblock is
   coded; Rust starts from 0.
3. **Signed overflow in `VP8ITransform`.** Coefficients above about 15,700 in
   magnitude overflow the `int` products of the horizontal pass; the dev and
   `checked` profiles report it. The DSP test bounds the coefficients at 15,000;
   the encoder's dequantized levels stay far below.
4. **Data races of the threaded alpha job on failure.** When the main loop fails
   with alpha and `thread_level > 0`, C's `StoreStats` runs while the worker may
   still write `pic->stats`; such cases do not request statistics.

`asan-ubsan` and `debug` oracle variants: the WP10 tests pass against both
(`LIBWEBP_ORACLE_DIR=build/oracle/<variant> cargo test --release --features oracle
--test 'wp10_*'`), so no other undefined behavior or failed upstream assertion
appeared on the compared inputs. The out-of-bounds read of deviation 9 lies
inside `VP8EncProba` and is not reported by the sanitizers.

## Upstream behavior preserved

- **`StatLoop` stops early for methods 0 to 2 with one segment.**
  `RefineUsingDistortion` never sets the header cost `rd->H`, so `OneStatPass`
  returns a partition-0 size of 0, which `StatLoop` treats as a failure: it returns
  before `FinalizeSkipProba`, `FinalizeTokenProbas` and `VP8CalculateLevelCosts`,
  and `VP8EncLoop` ignores the result. The skip probability stays unused and the
  token probabilities stay the defaults. With two segments the segment header cost
  is positive and the loop runs to its end. The edge-picture test covers both.
- **An abort during `StatLoop` does not stop `VP8EncLoop`**, which ignores
  `StatLoop`'s result and aborts at its own next progress report.
- **`ResetAllMBInfo` ignores the result of `WebPReportProgress`**: an abort is
  recorded on the picture, and `VP8EncAnalyze` succeeds.
- **`VP8RecordCoeffTokens` records the second token of category 5 with the
  probability index `base_id + 10` and the statistics slot 9.**
- **`VP8EstimateTokenSize` passes the bit as `token & (1 << 15)`** to `VP8BitCost`,
  which only tests it for zero.
- **`GeneratePartition0` measures its first position on the zeroed writer** before
  `VP8BitWriterInit`, which counts 8 bits.
- **`VP8CodeIntraModes` calls `VP8IteratorInit`**, which resets the top samples,
  the non-zero contexts and the diffusion errors of the encoder.
- **The alpha job adds its size to `pic->stats->coded_size`**, which `StoreStats`
  (WP13) overwrites with `enc->coded_size`.

## Mutation checks

Thirty-two mutations were tried, each against the WP10 tests (the cost probe for
the statistics threshold). Twenty-eight are caught: `SNS_TO_DQ`, the
trellis weights, the category-5 statistics slot, `MAX_ITERS_K_MEANS`, the inner
filter level of `GetILevel`, the color threshold of fast alpha filtering,
`DQ_LIMIT`, the majority of `SmoothSegmentMap`, an `FTransform` rounding constant,
`SKIP_PROBA_THRESHOLD`, `FLATNESS_PENALTY`, `min_disto`, the probability refresh
interval of the token loop and `MIN_COUNT`, the diffusion weight `C1`, the
top-right replication of `VP8IteratorRotateI4`, `FSTRENGTH_CUTOFF`, the JPEG
emulation's `amax`, `SegmentsAreEquivalent`, the alpha level mapping above quality
70, `try_filter_none`, the segment header's field width, the autofilter
improvement threshold, the filter-search step, `lambda_d_i4`, the threshold of
`FastMBAnalyze`, the overflow threshold of `VP8RecordStats` and the reset of
`max_edge`.

Two are equivalent: the clip of `dq_uv_dc` to [-15, 15] (`-4 * sns_strength / 100`
lies in [-4, 0] for every valid configuration), and `MIN_PAGE_SIZE` (the page size
changes where pages end, not the order in which tokens are emitted). Two survive,
because no generated picture reaches them: the initial header cost 211 of
`PickBestIntra4` (a one-unit change flips a decision only at a near tie), and the
fallback to uncompressed alpha when the compressed size equals the raw size
exactly.

## Notes for the packages that build on WP10

- **WP13 (`WebPEncode`, `StoreStats`).** The lossy branch, after the colorspace
  conversion and `WebPCleanupTransparentArea`, is the driver of
  `tests/wp10_encode.rs`: `let mut enc = init_vp8_encoder(config, pic)?` (the
  error is recorded on the picture), then `vp8_enc_analyze(&mut enc, config,
  pic)`, `vp8_enc_start_alpha(&mut enc, config, pic)`, `vp8_enc_loop` or, when
  `enc.use_tokens`, `vp8_enc_token_loop` (`&mut enc, config, pic`),
  `vp8_enc_finish_alpha(&mut enc, pic)`, `vp8_enc_write(&mut enc, pic)`, each
  only while the previous ones succeeded; then `StoreStats`,
  `vp8_enc_free_bit_writers(&mut enc)` on failure, and always
  `delete_vp8_encoder(enc, pic)`, whose result is and-ed in. `StoreStats` reads
  `enc.dqm[i].fstrength` and `.quant`, `enc.residual_bytes`, `enc.sse` and
  `enc.sse_count` (for `FinalizePSNR`), `enc.coded_size` and `enc.block_count`;
  the test's `store_stats` is the reference. The picture needs Y, U and V planes
  (`WEBP_YUV420`, plus A for `WEBP_YUV420A`) and a writer; the encoder reads the
  planes and writes them only with `show_compressed`, so a shared view can be
  encoded unless `show_compressed` is set. Stats are reset by `WebPEncode`
  itself. The progress hook stays on the calling thread, also while the threaded
  analysis runs. With `thread_level > 0` the alpha job's results reach the picture
  in `vp8_enc_finish_alpha`, or in `delete_vp8_encoder` when the loop failed (after
  `StoreStats` in C's order).
- **WP19 (SIMD encoder).** The dispatch functions are in `src/dsp/enc.rs`
  (`vp8f_transform`, `vp8i_transform`, `vp8f_transform_wht`, the predictors,
  `vp8sse*`, `vp8t_disto*`, `vp8_mean16x4`, `vp8_collect_histogram`, the three
  quantizers, the copies), `src/dsp/cost.rs` (`vp8_get_residual_cost`,
  `vp8_set_residual_coeffs`) and `src/dsp/quant.rs` (`is_flat`, for
  `IsFlat_NEON`). Blocks are slices from their first sample with stride `BPS`; the
  predictors' `left` is a buffer with the position of `left[0]` and `top` a slice,
  `VP8EncPredLuma4` reads `top[-5..8]` around its position, and on AArch64 the
  iterator's `i4_boundary` already has the 40 entries that `Intra4Preds_NEON`
  reads. The residual cost functions read `res.prob`, `res.costs.row(n, ctx)`,
  `res.first`, `res.last` and `res.coeffs`. `tests/wp10_dsp.rs` and
  `tests/wp10_cost.rs` give the parity inputs.
