# JPEG XL patches off: the size and quality cost, 2026-11-15

**Question.** The engine's lossy JPEG XL output is lossy distance 1.0 with
patches off, so a file equals `cjxl --patches=0 -e 7 -d 1` from the same rows
([ADR-0039](../adr/0039-jpeg-xl-output-through-the-workspace-encoder.md); owner
decision 2026-11-15, issue #236). The port cannot encode patches yet (encoder
step 5, planned after 1.0). How much larger, and how much worse, is that than
what the original encoder writes for text-like content (screenshots,
documents, UI)?

**Answer, for this corpus and this encoder version.** It depends on the size
of the image, because of how the original behaves at effort 7:

- **More than 8 groups of 256×256 pixels (roughly above 0.5 MP): no cost against
  the original's default.** `cjxl -e 7` with distance below 3 encodes such
  frames in streaming mode, and the streaming path skips the patch search
  (`CanDoStreamingEncoding` and `FindBestPatchDictionary` in the pinned
  source). The default and `--patches=0` files were **byte-identical on all
  12 of 12 generated text-like images of that size**, and so were the four
  photographs. The engine's output equals what `cjxl` writes by default.
- **At most 8 groups (for example 1024×512, 768×512, 912×512): patches were
  used by default in 6 of 6 generated text-like images, and turning them off
  makes the file 1.10× to 3.80× larger** (median 2.18× at distance 1.0, 2.11×
  at distance 2.0; the chart is the smallest effect, the web page the largest)
  **and lowers SSIMULACRA2 by 0.33 to 3.89 points at distance 1.0 (median
  1.78), by 0.62 to 9.33 at distance 2.0 (median 2.44)**. This is the case
  that applies to thumbnails and small screenshots, for example a resized
  screenshot.
- **Patches forced on (`--patches=1`, which also leaves streaming) shows what
  the port will leave on the table above 8 groups until step 5:** the same
  12 images shrink 1.01× to 9.49× (median 2.39× at distance 1.0) against the
  engine's output. Most of that is patches (the pure patch effect, with
  streaming off in both, is median 2.35×); about 1% to 5% is leaving streaming. The
  original does not do this by default at effort 7, so it is an opportunity,
  not a regression against `cjxl`.
- **Photographs: no difference** in size or quality, with or without forcing
  patches (forcing only leaves streaming, which saves about 1% to 2% on the two
  large photographs and nothing on the small ones).

The byte identity with pinned libjxl, which is the v1.0 quality gate
(ADR-0041), is unaffected: this record measures the original against itself
with one switch.

## Method

- **Encoder, decoder, scorer.** The pinned original libjxl, submodule
  `codecs/libjxl-rs/upstream/libjxl` at `a16d32d0` (the
  `v0.12-snapshot-50-ga16d32d0` snapshot reported as v0.13.0), built Release
  with the dependency selection of
  [`scripts/bench/ci-perf/codec_jxl.sh`](../../scripts/bench/ci-perf/codec_jxl.sh)
  plus the devtools switch that provides `ssimulacra2`
  ([`build_libjxl.sh`](../../scripts/bench/jxl-patches/build_libjxl.sh)),
  Apple Clang 21.0.0, AArch64 (NEON, default Highway targets), Darwin 27.2,
  `-j4`. Version strings from the tools:
  `cjxl v0.13.0 a16d32d [_NEON_] {AppleClang 21.0.0.21000401}`,
  `djxl v0.13.0 a16d32d [_NEON_] {AppleClang 21.0.0.21000401}`.
  `ssimulacra2` is the tool of the same build
  (`tools/ssimulacra2_main.cc`; it prints a score, not a version string).
- **Encodes.** For every image and distance 1.0 and 2.0, effort 7,
  `--num_threads=0` (serial, so the bytes are reproducible), PPM input (sRGB),
  bare codestream, four times: default settings; `--patches=0` (what the
  engine writes); `--patches=1` (search forced on); and `--buffering=0
  --patches=0` (streaming off, patches off; separates leaving streaming from
  patches). No timing was taken and none is reported.
- **Quality.** Each output is decoded with `djxl` (8-bit PPM) and scored
  against the source PPM with SSIMULACRA2 (higher is better; about 90 and
  above is visually very close, 70 to 90 is good).
- **Patches used.** The tools print no flag. A default encode counts as using
  patches when it differs from the `--patches=0` file; with patches off the
  frame header says so, so equal bytes (compared by SHA-256 in the JSON) mean
  none were written. `jxlinfo -v` of this build does not show patches.
- **Why 8 groups.** In the pinned source, `cjxl -e 7` with distance below 3
  and default buffering streams a frame with more than 8 groups of 256×256
  (`CanDoStreamingEncoding`, `lib/jxl/enc_frame.cc`), and the streaming path
  skips the patch, spline and dot search (`lib/jxl/enc_heuristics.cc`).
  The port's public encoder is documented the same way in ADR-0039 ("one-shot
  up to 8 groups and streaming above"). The group count of each image is in
  the tables; the size classes below follow it.
- **Reproduce.**
  `WORK=<dir> PYTHON=<python with pillow and numpy> bash scripts/bench/jxl-patches/run.sh`
  builds the tools, generates the corpus, measures and writes
  `<dir>/results.json`; `python3 scripts/bench/jxl-patches/tables.py
  <dir>/results.json` prints the tables below. The raw results of this record
  are [`results-2026-11-15.json`](../../scripts/bench/jxl-patches/results-2026-11-15.json).

## Corpus

Eighteen generated text-like images and four photographs, 22 in all, PPM,
8-bit sRGB. [`make_corpus.py`](../../scripts/bench/jxl-patches/make_corpus.py)
draws the text-like images with Pillow 13.0.0 (FreeType 2.14.3) and NumPy
2.5.4 in a Python 3.15 venv; nothing was installed system-wide and nothing was
downloaded. Text is set in the font bundled with Pillow
(`ImageFont.load_default(size)`), in sizes from 12.5 to 46 px at 1× scale, regular
(and stroked bold at 2× scale), with antialiasing on and off (four images), light and dark themes, and
pseudo-random words from a fixed seed. Pixel dimensions and SHA-256 of every
file are in the script's `corpus.json`; the generator output was identical in
the two runs made for this record.

### Where patches made no difference

- Every image of more than 8 groups, in the default comparison, with and
  without antialiasing, light and dark, 1 MP and 4 MP: identical bytes (24 of
  24 encodes of text-like images, 8 of 8 of photographs).
- All photographs, in every comparison, including forced: the heuristic found
  nothing (forced and streaming-off files are identical).
- The scan simulation (`document-scan-1mp`): forced patches found nothing
  either (1.007 and 1.011 from leaving streaming alone); grain and blur make
  repeated glyphs unequal, which the patch search needs.
- The charts gain least (1.09× to 1.17×), presumably because their bars are
  flat areas that VarDCT already codes cheaply and they hold little text
  (a guess, not tested).

## Limits of the measurement

- **A small generated corpus**: 18 text-like images in six kinds, one
  typeface family, pseudo-random words (more letter repetition than prose,
  less than a real page with many fonts, but the letter shapes repeat in a way
  real UI text does too). Real screenshots with photographs inside, gradients,
  subpixel-rendered text or many typefaces will fall between these numbers and
  the photograph rows. The kind summaries of the small class rest on one image
  per kind; read the range, not the median, there.
- **One effort and two distances.** Effort 7 and distances 1.0 and 2.0 only.
  At effort 8 and above `cjxl` does not stream and its default does search
  for patches (observed once by hand on `terminal-light-4mp` at distance 1: 59.7 kB
  by default against 235.9 kB with `--patches=0` at effort 8; not in the JSON).
  Distance 3 and above is outside the port's admitted profile.
- **Patch use is inferred from a byte difference**, not read from a flag.
- **AArch64 only.** The encoder bytes are deterministic per target and kernel
  level (ADR-0039); x86-64 was not measured, so exact byte counts may differ
  by a small amount there. The ratios should not.
- **SSIMULACRA2 is one metric.** It was the only scorer used; no Butteraugli
  or human viewing. Decoded with `djxl` to 8-bit PPM, so 8-bit clipping is
  part of the score. PSNR was not computed.
- **Dots and splines** are not changed by `--patches=0`; dots need distance
  3 or more in this version, so they do not apply here.
- **The photographs are small or JPEG-origin** (three of four were decoded
  from JPEG fixtures with Pillow's libjpeg, so a different Pillow build may
  change their pixels slightly) and two are about 0.12 MP.
- **Size only, no timing.** Encode times were not recorded.
- **The engine's own file** was not re-encoded here; the equivalence of the
  engine's output with `cjxl --patches=0 -e 7 -d 1` is the subject of the
  encoder gate (ADR-0039, ADR-0041), not of this record.
