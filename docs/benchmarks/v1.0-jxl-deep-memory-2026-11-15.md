# Lossless JPEG XL encode memory above 8 bits, 2026-11-15

**Memory, not timing**: peak resident memory of a fresh process; no timing
is reported. Evidence and tools:
[evidence-2026-11-15-jxl-deep-memory](evidence-2026-11-15-jxl-deep-memory/README.md).

## Question

Since the owner decision of 2026-11-15 the JPEG XL output depth follows the
input (ADR-0021 amendment), so the lossless sink hands the encoder `uint16`
rows for inputs deeper than 8 bits. The sink's reservation
(`src/jxl_sink.rs`, issue #223) was measured for 8-bit rows. What does a
deep lossless encode need, measured rather than derived?

## Method

The method of [the 8-bit record](v1.0-jxl-lossless-chunked-memory-2026-11-15.md):
Apple Silicon, macOS, shared machine, every run under the gate lock, one
fresh process per run, two runs, the larger peak RSS (`/usr/bin/time -l`).
Port: the bridge's `encode_pnm` example (the sink's call). Original: the
pinned scalar `cjxl`. Inputs: a 16-bit photograph mirrored to 0.5, 4.2,
6, 12.2 and 24 MP, opaque and with alpha, and the same samples' high bytes
as an 8-bit control. Bytes: the port's file equals `cjxl`'s for all 40
cases (8 and 16 bits, RGB and RGBA, one-shot and chunked).

## Reading

- 16-bit rows cost more than 8-bit rows of the same content: 37.8 against
  25.5 B/px (RGB) and 49.7 against 28.8 (RGBA) at 24 MP; 73.3 against 56.2
  and 97.3 against 72.9 at one DC group (2048x2048). The input copy doubles
  and the Modular tokens grow with the deeper residuals.
- The port stays within the original's figure except at 24 MP RGBA,
  16 bits (1,192 MB against 1,154 MB, +3.3 %), 6 MP RGBA, 16 bits (+0.5 %),
  and 512x1024 RGB, 8 bits (+0.2 %).
- 16 bits is the widest case of the `u16` buffers; 9 to 15 bits use the same
  planes with smaller residuals, so the 16-bit terms are the reservation
  for every depth above 8.

## Reservation model for samples above 8 bits (`src/jxl_sink.rs`)

As for 8 bits, the peak minus the input the measured process held (6 or
8 B/px), on top of the engine's 8 B/px `u16` frame:

| Term | Opaque RGB | RGBA |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| one-shot, at most 8 groups: per pixel | 88 | 107 |
| chunked: per pixel of the frame | 25 | 32 |
| chunked: per pixel of one DC group | 71 | 99 |
| constant | 12 MiB | 12 MiB |

`jxl_output_tests::input_frame_and_measured_lossless_peaks_are_reserved`
checks that every deep peak above fits the frame plus the reservation at
10, 12 and 16 bits. Under the default 1 GiB budget the largest deep
lossless output is 28,272,715 px opaque and 20,185,088 px with alpha.

## Finding on the 8-bit model

The 8-bit control of this content at 12.2 MP with alpha peaked at 611 MB
(50.1 B/px), above the 8-bit model's 45.1 B/px there (fitted on other
photographs on 2026-11-15); every other 8-bit control fits. The 8-bit terms
are not changed in this package (they are not this content's model); the
gap is reported for a follow-up of issue #223.
